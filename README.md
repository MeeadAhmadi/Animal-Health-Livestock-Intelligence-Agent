Livestock Intelligence & Early-Warning System

An intelligence platform for an industrial pig-farming organization that continuously monitors the animal-health, veterinary, livestock-production, regulatory, scientific, technology, and market ecosystem.

The system is designed to answer a more useful question than:

> "What happened today?"

Instead:

> **What is changing in the world that could matter to this company, its pig farms, animal health, operations, costs, compliance, technology roadmap, research strategy, or future competitiveness?**

The project is intentionally being developed as a **local-first Demo/MVP** before any production deployment.

---

# 1. Project Vision

This project is not intended to become a conventional news aggregator.

It is a **Livestock Intelligence & Early-Warning System** combining:

- Global information collection
- Scientific and veterinary intelligence
- Government and regulatory monitoring
- Livestock industry and market monitoring
- Company-specific context
- Evidence ranking
- Relevance and impact scoring
- Deduplication and event correlation
- Emerging-topic detection
- Early-warning signals
- Historical intelligence
- Evidence-based article analysis
- A domain-bounded AI assistant

The initial target domain is **industrial pig production**.

Primary areas of interest include:

- Pig and piglet health
- Veterinary medicine
- Swine diseases
- Antimicrobial resistance
- Antibiotics and alternatives
- Vaccines
- Diagnostics
- New treatments
- Feed and nutrition
- Biosecurity
- Animal welfare
- Farm technology
- Automation
- Precision livestock farming
- Genetics and reproduction
- Manure and environmental management
- EU and national regulations
- Market developments
- Research and R&D
- Emerging risks
- Emerging technologies

---

# 2. Core Objectives

The system has two primary intelligence outputs.

## 2.1 Daily Intelligence

A daily intelligence brief containing the most important signals detected during the collection period.

The brief should prioritize:

1. Critical alerts
2. High-priority intelligence
3. Emerging signals
4. Research developments
5. Regulatory developments
6. Disease developments
7. Technology developments
8. Market developments
9. Relevant background information

The objective is:

> **High signal-to-noise ratio, not maximum article volume.**

---

## 2.2 Longitudinal Intelligence

All useful information must be stored as structured historical data.

This enables:

- Trend detection
- Topic velocity
- Emerging-topic detection
- Geographic comparison
- Scientific momentum
- Policy momentum
- Industry momentum
- Market intelligence
- Research strategy
- Competitive intelligence
- Early-warning detection
- Policy → Research → Market mapping

The system should become more valuable over time because its historical evidence base grows.
